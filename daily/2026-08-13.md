### Sample Queries (Planned): PostGIS SQL:

```
SELECT s.id
FROM schools s
JOIN parks p ON ST_Contains(p.geom, s.geom);
```

**GeoPandas Python:**

```
schools_in_parks = gpd.sjoin(schools, parks, predicate='within')
```
